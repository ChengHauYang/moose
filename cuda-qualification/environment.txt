=== device scope of the qualified CUDA-NEML2 workloads ===
On this CUDA build the Kokkos execution/memory space is compile-time Cuda/CudaSpace, so Kokkos
kernels and buffers are ALWAYS on the GPU; --compute-device selects only where NEML2/libtorch
(and the solver) run. For the qualified --compute-device=cuda runs:
  - Kokkos assembly/gather: GPU
  - NEML2/libtorch constitutive evaluation: GPU
  - PETSc linear/nonlinear solve: CPU
PETSc remains on the CPU for these workloads. This is NOT a full-GPU PETSc qualification; only the
Kokkos assembly and NEML2 constitutive evaluation are qualified on the device.
